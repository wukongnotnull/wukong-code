## Methodology

This matched the RED isolation protocol: fresh Codex desktop child contexts,
`fork_turns: none`, neutral task names, and no tests, plan, specification,
rubric, prior evidence, or expected answer in their prompts. Each child could
inspect only the Product Design skill files required to route and could not
edit, generate images, browse, or deploy. Harness/model version and identifiers
were not exposed by the runtime. Every response was manually reviewed.
